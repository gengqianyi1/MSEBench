| Case | Expected raw score | Expected failure |
|---|---:|---|

Scoring bounds are 0–100, pass threshold 60, reward = raw/100. The five public dimensions are 20 points each and private hard gates are unchanged. Replay tolerance is `1e-9` plus exact result/failure/property evidence. The 100-point Oracle and three adversarial cases calibrate the deterministic surrogate only; the task remains non-leaderboard.
