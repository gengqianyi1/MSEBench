# Private suite, Oracle, thresholds and calibration

The fixtures are verifier-only and generated by `scripts/validate_tasks_10_13_protocol.py`.

| Case | Expected raw score | Expected failure |
|---|---:|---|
| `oracle_six_step_route` | 100.0 | none |
| `invalid_empty_route` | 0.0 | parse/process/target gates |
| `invalid_unknown_reaction` | 17.8571 | reaction library, step match, final target |
| `invalid_wrong_final` | 54.1667 | `final_equals_target` |

Scoring bounds are 0–100, pass threshold 60, reward = raw/100; components are 35/25/40. Replay tolerance is `1e-9` plus exact failure/per-step evidence. Calibration covers the 2-step and 6-step boundaries, reaction-name/order equivalence in the legacy private tests, the perfect Oracle and partial-credit invalid routes. The task remains non-leaderboard.

The three negative submission fixtures under `task_snapshot/invalid_submissions/` are separate from the private grader boundary and exercise parser, unknown-reaction and wrong-final-product paths.
